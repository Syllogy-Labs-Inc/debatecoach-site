Debate Coach

Help and support

For people using Debate Coach on iPhone or Android.

Ask your coach first

For anything about practising, your coach in the app knows your reports and can explain any mark. Open the Coach tab, or in the Conversation view just say it.

Write to us

For the app itself, your account, a payment or anything else: hello@debatecoach.app. A person answers, usually within a day.

Common questions

  • My practice has no score. The coach needs about thirty seconds of English to give marks. Under that, or in another language, the report says so and nothing is charged.
  • The microphone does not work. Open your phone's Settings, find Debate Coach and allow the microphone. The app asks once, the first time you practise.
  • I bought credits and they have not arrived. They usually land within a minute. If not, open You, then Credits, and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same account you bought with.
  • Cancelling a plan. Plans are managed by your store: on iPhone under Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your month runs to its end and pack credits stay.
  • Refunds. Apple and Google handle refunds for their purchases. Write to us if you need help asking.
  • Deleting my account. In the app: You, Privacy and your data, Delete my account. It removes your account, recordings, transcripts and reports. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on the account and we delete it for you within a few days.
