Debate Coach

Delete your account

Everything in the account goes with it.

In the app

Open You (your mark at the top right in the Conversation view, or the You tab), then Privacy and your data, then Delete my account, then Delete everything. It is done at once.

What goes

Your account and sign-in, your recordings, the transcripts of what you said, every report, your chat with the coach, your settings and your streak. A subscription bought through Apple or Google is not cancelled by this; cancel it in your store's subscription settings so it does not renew.

If you cannot open the app

Email hello@debatecoach.app from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We confirm by reply and delete it within a few days.

What stays

A record of purchases, which Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep under their own rules, and usage figures that are not tied to you.
